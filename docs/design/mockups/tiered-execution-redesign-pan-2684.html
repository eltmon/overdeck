Supervisor

▸Standing reviewer
Wakes on commit events and reviews the diff against the task's acceptance criteria. Findings block the foreman. Required whenever tiers are configured — to run unsupervised, disable tiered execution.
Owns inspection — pan inspect routes here instead of spawning an ephemeral inspector

Kind overrides

All kinds follow difficulty routing.

Advanced

▸Commit feed
Every standing tier hears every commit (keeps sessions warm, keeps replay deterministic). These knobs only remove noise or grant listeners a limited voice — they never filter by relevance.
▸Escalation
On a supervisor BLOCKED verdict, a verification failure, or a blown time budget, the task's difficulty is promoted one step up the ladder — after the configured retries at its current tier, and never past the promotion cap. At the cap (or at expert), it blocks and surfaces to you.
Enabled
Floundering budget (minutes) — promote when a task exceeds its difficulty's budget; blank = trigger inactive
▸Replay & compaction
A tier session that dies or fills its context is restarted and re-fed the issue history from durable commits — replay IS the compaction strategy.
