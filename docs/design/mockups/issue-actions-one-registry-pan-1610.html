Issue actions — one registry, every surface

PAN-1610. All four issue surfaces already call the same useIssueActions hook — the drift is in presentation: the cockpit invented its own grouping, the rail flattens everything and hardcodes extras, the board and drawer share a third "hybrid toolbar" shape. This proposal makes the registry own grouping + ordering + surface extras, and reduces each surface to a declared presentation mode. Change the phase below and watch all four update from the single source.

What the registry gains (and surfaces lose)

Grouping and order move into the registry. GROUP_ORDER/GROUP_LABELS leave IssueMissionControl.tsx:86-108 (delivered by PAN-2661) so every presentation renders the same sections in the same order. A surface declares only its mode: grouped-panel, grouped-context, or primary-strip (phase-primary buttons + a grouped overflow), plus optional pinned actions (the drawer pins Merge).

Surface extras become registry entries with a surface tag. Open State Dir / View JSONL / the duplicate Deep Wipe stop being hardcoded JSX in FeatureItem.tsx:734-755; they register with surfaces: ['rail-session'], inherit confirm flows, and appear in the parity test like everything else.

Drift becomes a red test, not a bug report. A parity test renders each mode against the registry and asserts: identical item sets, identical gating, destructive-last, and no surface-local action definitions outside the registry module. The flat/grouped split that motivated this issue can't quietly return.

Scope note

This lands after PAN-2499 (one IssueView family = fewer mounts to convert) and after PAN-2661 (which moves grouping into the registry and ships the grouped context menu). What remains here: convert the cockpit panel to consume the registry grouping, re-express the board/drawer hybrid as primary-strip mode, registry-register the surface extras, and add the parity test. The action set, gating predicates, dialogs host, and confirm flows do not change anywhere in this work.
