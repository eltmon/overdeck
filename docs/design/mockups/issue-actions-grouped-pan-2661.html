PAN-2661. The rail's right-click menu dumps all 44 registry actions in declaration order under one "Issue actions" label (FeatureItem.tsx:711-714 flattens primary+secondary+overflow). The registry already carries group metadata, and the cockpit's ⌘ Actions panel already renders it grouped — this proposal gives the context menu the same taxonomy. The proposed menu is interactive: switch phases and watch it regate.

Deep Wipe
  • 44 items, one label, declaration order. Planning, agent recovery, artifacts, and destruction interleave in whatever order they were registered in issueActions.ts — you scan the whole list every time, and the group metadata that could organize it is thrown away by the flatten.
  • Destructive actions are scattered, not last. "Remove review sessions & reset" sits between review and agent items; "Wipe" and "Destroy workspace" sit mid-list with "Open" right below them. Red styling is the only separation.
  • Deep Wipe exists twice. The registry's "Wipe" AND a hardcoded surface extra at the bottom (FeatureItem.tsx:750-755) — same irreversible operation, two entries, different confirm flows.
  • Mostly-disabled noise. In any given phase roughly two-thirds of items are gray. They're kept (correctly — disabled-with-reason is good UX) but with no grouping the gray majority buries the few live actions.
  • An orphan ships in the list. reviewTest (group "preserved") is a legacy compat entry surfaced nowhere deliberately — it needs a disposition, not a menu slot.

Proposed — the cockpit's taxonomy, phase-first

One registry, one grouping. The menu opens with For this phase — the same phase-primary actions useIssueActions already computes — then the registry groups in cockpit order. Disabled items stay visible and keep their reason tooltips (hover them). Danger is separated, last, and collapsed behind one row so a fast scan can't end in an irreversible click.

Issue phase:

What you're seeing. The item set, gating, and confirm flows are untouched — this is the same 44-action registry (ISSUE_ACTIONS), rendered with the group metadata it already carries plus the cockpit's GROUP_ORDER. Empty groups hide. The phase chip and counts keep you oriented; keyboard: ↑↓ move, ⏎ run, type to filter (spec'd, not mocked).

Surface extras become a labeled section. "This session" carries Open State Dir / View JSONL. The hardcoded Deep Wipe is deleted — the registry's Wipe in Danger is the single entry (one confirm flow).

Danger is a disclosure. Collapsed by default with a count; expanding shows the destructive set, red, with their existing typed-confirm gates. Reopen (safe) stays in the group but unstyled.

Hover a grayed item to see its disabled reason here (mirrors today's tooltips).

What changed, and why

The grouping already existed — it was just discarded. Every action in issueActions.ts declares a group; the cockpit's ⌘ Actions panel renders those groups; the context menu flattens them away. The fix is to move GROUP_ORDER/GROUP_LABELS from IssueMissionControl.tsx:86-108 into the registry module and have BOTH presentations consume it — which is also the first half of PAN-1610 (one registry, no drift).

Phase-first beats alphabet-first. The hook already computes phase-primary actions; putting them at the top means the two or three things you actually came for are under the cursor, while the full grouped set stays one scan away. Disabled items remain visible with reasons — discoverability is a feature; noise was the bug, and grouping fixes the noise.

Exactly one way to destroy things. Danger collapsed + the duplicate hardcoded Deep Wipe removed = every irreversible action lives in one labeled place with one confirm flow.
