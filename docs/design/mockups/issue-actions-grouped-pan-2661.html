Deep Wipe
  • 44 items, one label, declaration order. FeatureItem.tsx:711-714 flattens primary+secondary+overflow, discarding the group metadata every action already declares in issueActions.ts.
  • Destructive actions are scattered, not last. "Remove review sessions & reset" sits mid-list; "Wipe" and "Destroy workspace" have "Open" directly beneath them. Red text is the only separation.
  • Deep Wipe exists twice — the registry's "Wipe" and a hardcoded extra at FeatureItem.tsx:750-755, with different confirm flows.
  • Mostly-disabled noise. In a typical phase two-thirds of items are gray; without grouping the gray buries the live few.
  • An orphan ships in the list — reviewTest (group "preserved") is a legacy compat entry that needs a disposition, not a menu slot.
  • Nothing explains itself. Labels assume you already know Overdeck's pipeline; a newcomer can't tell "Reset stalled review state" from "Remove review sessions & reset" without reading source.

Proposed — phase-first, grouped, self-explaining

The menu opens with For this phase (the registry's own PHASE_PRIMARY_KEYS), then the groups in cockpit order. Disabled items stay visible with their reason on hover. Danger is collapsed, last. The Explain toggle is the newcomer layer: every action grows a one-line plain-language description and its pan verb — proposed as a required description field on the registry so all surfaces (and the docs) share one copy.

What changed, and why

The grouping already existed — it was discarded at render time. Move GROUP_ORDER/GROUP_LABELS from IssueMissionControl.tsx:86-108 into issueActions.ts and render the context menu from it. That's also the first half of PAN-1610.

Phase-first beats scan-everything. The phase → primary-actions map already exists in the registry (PHASE_PRIMARY_KEYS, 11 phases). Surfacing it as the top section puts the two-or-three things you came for under the cursor; note INPUT, STUCK, and CHANGES_REQUESTED are phases too — the menu leads with recovery actions exactly when an issue needs rescuing.

Exactly one way to destroy things. Danger collapsed + hardcoded Deep Wipe deleted = every irreversible action in one labeled place with one typed-confirm flow.
