#### Fixed

- **`npm run typecheck` waits in the admission queue once:** the whole typecheck now takes one gate admission and runs its lanes one after another inside it, instead of queueing again before each of its 12 lanes. Under memory pressure a typecheck no longer takes close to an hour, and `npm run lint` does the same for its Effect diagnostics lanes. ([#5617](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5617))
