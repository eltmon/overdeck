#### Fixed

- **Open epics no longer look shipped after one slice merges:** the issue view used to show an open epic as Merged, tick every pipeline step, say "Shipped — this change is on main" and offer Close out as soon as one slice PR merged. Now the epic keeps its real state, the merged PR shows as a merged slice, Close out stays off, the merge agent no longer labels the epic `merged` or `verifying-on-main`, and `pan close` refuses an open epic unless the operator passes `--accept-merged`. ([#5279](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5279))
- **Long issue titles wrap in the issue view:** the Stage view's header title wraps to up to three lines instead of ending in an ellipsis. The drawer header still truncates. ([#5279](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5279))
