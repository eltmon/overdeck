#### Fixed

- **A fresh Flywheel start keeps the previous run's open items:** starting the Flywheel now keeps the previous run's conversation under a retired name instead of deleting it. `pan flywheel status --json` reports that run's last tick, in-flight list and open needs-you items under `previousRun`, and the Flywheel page keeps listing those needs-you items until the new run's first tick. The new loop drains the old in-flight list first and carries the open items forward. The **Start fresh** confirm now says what the new loop keeps and recommends **Resume** when the paused run did not stop cleanly. ([#5666](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5666))
