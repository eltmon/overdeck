#### New

- **Reset-first CLIProxy routing and an accounts panel:** when CLIProxy holds two or more logins for a provider, the host dashboard now sends new sessions to the account whose weekly quota resets soonest, so quota is not lost at the reset; running sessions stay on their account. Settings → Providers shows a CLIProxy accounts panel with each account's usage windows, reset times and rank. ([#5278](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5278))
