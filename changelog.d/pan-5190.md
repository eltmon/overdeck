#### New

- **File actions in the explorer:** create, rename, duplicate, move by drag and delete files and folders in an issue workspace's file tree. Deleted files go to the Overdeck trash, and `Ctrl/Cmd+Z` undoes the last action. Drop files from your desktop onto a folder to upload them, or drag rows into a conversation composer to insert their paths. ([#5190](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5190))
