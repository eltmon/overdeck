#### Fixed

- **Quality gates no longer wait forever under sustained CPU pressure:** a long, steady CPU load from outside Overdeck used to hold every quality gate, local Vitest run and typecheck lane for as long as it lasted. The gate at the head of the admission queue now starts alone once it has waited on CPU for `resources.quality_gate_cpu_bypass_minutes` (default 10) and no other gate holds a slot. The CPU pressure start threshold is now configurable as `resources.quality_gate_psi_start_avg10` (default 25). Both settings live in `~/.overdeck/config.yaml` and reach queued gates on their next poll. ([#5520](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5520))
