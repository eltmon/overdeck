#### Fixed

- **Work agents come back after a host crash:** a work agent whose terminal pane died without a recorded stop, for example after a host crash or reboot, is now resumed automatically once it has been confirmed dead on two consecutive deacon checks. Paused, suspended, troubled and operator-stopped agents, closed or merged issues, a frozen deacon and `--no-resume` boots are left alone. Resumes run one at a time and wait for memory headroom. A failed resume raises one Needs-you item instead of retrying. Deacon routine actions are now written to `deacon.log`. ([#5354](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5354))
