#### New

- **What a conversation changed:** `GET /api/conversations/:name/changes` lists a conversation's commits, with pushed state and the turn that made each one, and its uncommitted edits, with per-line attribution for Claude Code conversations. ([#5288](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5288))
- **Who made a project's recent commits:** `GET /api/projects/:projectKey/changes` lists recent default-branch commits with the conversation or agent that made each one, including squash-merged work. ([#5288](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5288))

#### Improved

- **Project spend includes conversations:** a project's spend now counts the cost of its conversations, not only its issues, and "today" starts at local midnight on the dashboard machine instead of midnight UTC. ([#5288](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5288))
