#### New

- **Add skills to a running conversation:** `pan skills add <conversation> --pack <id>`, the `add_skills` MCP tool and the **Add skills…** item in the conversation menus load skill packs into a conversation that is already running. Claude Code loads them in place with `/reload-plugins` and keeps its context; Codex and launch-wired packs restart and resume the conversation with its history. ([#5465](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5465))
