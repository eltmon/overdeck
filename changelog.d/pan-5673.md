#### Fixed

- **A deliberate stop survives a dashboard restart:** a conversation you stopped or paused on purpose, including the Flywheel, now stays stopped after `pan reload` or any other dashboard restart. Overdeck stores the stop on the conversation, so neither the restart recovery offer nor the Flywheel watchdog's resume after a reboot undoes it. Resuming or starting the conversation clears it. ([#5673](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5673))
