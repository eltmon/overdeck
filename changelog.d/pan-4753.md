#### New

- **Merge train switch on the Flywheel page:** the Flywheel header has a real **Merge train** switch next to Auto-pickup and Require UAT. With it on, normal-priority PRs wait and merge together as one batch once the batch's CI is green: when 3 are ready, or when the oldest has waited 30 minutes. Strikes and issues labeled `critical`, `blocks-main`, `P0`, `urgent`, or both `substrate-improvement` and `bug` still merge on their own right away. If a batch's CI fails, one PR is dropped and the rest merge without it. A PR only you may merge (held for UAT, an auto-merge you cancelled, or a Deft human-merge policy) gets its own batch for you and never holds the others back. Batching needs a CI workflow named `CI` that runs on `uat/**` pushes; without one, a batch waits at most 10 minutes, then its PRs merge one at a time. Batch size and wait are in **Settings → Merge Train**. ([#4753](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4753))

#### Fixed

- **Turning the merge train off no longer stops merges:** with the train off, every ready PR merges on its own as soon as it is green, as the switch promises. Before, off stopped automatic merging altogether. ([#4753](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4753))
