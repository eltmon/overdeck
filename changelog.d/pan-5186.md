#### New

- **File explorer in the Stage:** the **Files** button on a project's home and on an issue tab now opens a live tree of that checkout, colored by git status, with quick open (Ctrl/Cmd+P), content search (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+F) and a right-click menu to copy paths and stage, unstage or discard changes. The **Changed** filter replaces the old changed-files list. ([#5186](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5186))
