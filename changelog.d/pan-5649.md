#### Improved

- **Opening a stopped workspace app starts it:** when a workspace's frontend was stopped after being unused, its app URL now shows a short "Starting it…" page, starts the containers and loads the app once it answers. Scripts and agents get `503` with `Retry-After` until then. The Command Deck issue row shows a **start app** button while the app is stopped. The dashboard adds the Traefik route itself when it starts. ([#5649](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5649))
