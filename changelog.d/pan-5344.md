#### Fixed

- **A browser on your own machine always signs in:** linking a machine, pairing a device or adding a remote address turns on **Require a token to sign in** by itself, and that used to lock you out of your own dashboard with "Dashboard session could not be established (HTTP 401)" once your session cookie was gone. The setting now refuses only requests a reverse proxy forwarded; `localhost:3011` and `overdeck.localhost` sign in with no token either way. ([#5344](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5344))
