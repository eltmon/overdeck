#### New

- **See the whole merge train on one card per project:** the Flywheel rail and each project's Home now show what is merging, what waits for the next batch and when it assembles, what is blocked and why (failing checks, a conflict, a draft, requested changes, open review threads), the strike PRs at the front of the train, and the last five merges, including ones made outside the train. Each row expands to its branch, PR link, merge policy, blockers, reviewer UAT notes and a nine-step merge tracker. ([#5277](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5277))

#### Improved

- **Voided batches say why:** a batch that was replaced or voided now stays visible as an "Older batch" row with one sentence of reason, for example "PAN-1 was merged on its own, outside the batch". ([#5277](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5277))
