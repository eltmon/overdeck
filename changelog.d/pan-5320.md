#### Fixed

- **GitLab and multi-repo projects get a merge train again:** the merge train's ready set and queue now read GitLab merge requests and every member repo of a polyrepo project, so Mind Your Now's ready features assemble into UAT batches instead of the train sitting empty. A polyrepo feature is ready when every one of its pull or merge requests is approved, green and mergeable, and a forge outage no longer reads as "nothing ready", so a live batch survives it.
