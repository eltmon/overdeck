#### Improved

- **The sidebar logo shows when Overdeck is busy:** while an agent is running or a conversation is mid-turn, the satellite in the Overdeck mark orbits its ring, like the logo animation in the Overdeck videos. When everything is idle it rests in the normal logo pose. It stays still if your system asks for reduced motion. ([#5280](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5280))

#### Fixed

- **Home project menu no longer cut off:** the "In: project" menu under the Home composer opened upward and was clipped by the page header. It now opens below or above the chip, whichever fits. ([#5280](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5280))
