#### Fixed

- **Quality gates no longer wait on CPU that is free:** a workload pinned to a few cores, such as encodes run with `taskset`, raised CPU pressure readings and held every local gate in the admission queue while most cores sat idle. Gates now treat CPU pressure readings as contention only once the whole machine is at least 50% busy. ([#5589](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5589))
