#### Improved

- **The Order books page shows which book is live:** books are grouped Active, Queued, Drafts and Archived, and the selected book says in one sentence what its phase means. Each book has one issue list with full titles and one plain status per issue, such as Working, In review or Waiting for a free slot. Labels replace the old jargon. ([#5597](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5597))
- **Archive a book or move it back to draft:** the page and the new `pan orders unqueue`, `pan orders archive` and `pan orders unarchive` commands move a book between phases, and `pan orders list` shows each book's phase. ([#5597](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5597))

#### Fixed

- **Landed issues no longer block a book:** a queued book whose issues have partly or fully landed can be started, and its landed issues are no longer shown as errors. ([#5597](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5597))
