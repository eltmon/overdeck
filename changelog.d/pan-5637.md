#### Fixed

- **Polyrepo merge train and repos without CI:** a polyrepo issue that changes a member repo with no CI pipeline was never ready to merge. Set `ci: none` on that repo in `projects.yaml`; the merge train then counts its merge requests' missing pipeline as passing, while failing or pending pipelines still block.
