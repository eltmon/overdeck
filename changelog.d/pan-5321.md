#### New

- **Set an issue's merge policy from the dashboard:** the Auto / Hold toggle on merge train rows, the issue header and Awaiting Merge now sets the issue's `auto-merge` or `hold-for-uat` label on GitHub-tracked projects. Clicking the pressed choice removes the label so the issue follows its project again. On other trackers the toggle stays read-only. Agents cannot change these labels. ([#5321](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5321))

#### Improved

- **Merge batch checks the UAT stack first:** when a batch needs UAT and its UAT stack is not running, **Merge batch** refuses and says the stack's state. Start the stack and test, or choose **Merge anyway…** and give a reason, which each member's history records. ([#3176](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/3176))
