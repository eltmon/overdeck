#### New

- **Safe shutdown:** `pan shutdown` (or "Shut down Overdeck…" in the agents menu) lets every agent finish its current tool call, stops everything, and tells you when it is safe to reboot. A banner shows what is still running, and after the restart Needs you offers to resume the stopped sessions. ([#5342](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5342))

#### Fixed

- **Emergency stop from the command palette:** it now opens the stop confirmation instead of calling a route that did not exist. ([#5342](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5342))
