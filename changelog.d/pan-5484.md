#### Fixed

- **`pan sync` prunes its backups:** each sync now keeps the newest 10 backups in `~/.overdeck/backups` (set `backup_keep` under `[sync]` in `config.toml`) instead of keeping every one forever. Retention skips `dist-rollbacks` and other named folders and never deletes `pan context detach` backups, and `pan backup clean --keep` now rejects a count that is not a whole number instead of deleting every backup. ([#5484](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5484))
