#### Improved

- **The Flywheel page is a live lane board:** the Status tab now shows every in-flight issue as a card in its lane (Plan, Build, Checks, Review, Merge, Deploy, Done today), with the full title, time in stage, cost, who drives it, its agent, and one plain sentence on why it is not moving, such as a typecheck queued for memory or CI failing on its PR. Needs-you items are action cards with why and since, a jump to the message, and a real control where one applies (the test-removal waiver, Start, Open). A health strip shows main CI, the deployed build against it, the gate queue, the next train batch and the last release, and a timeline lists what merged, deployed and released today. The header counts every card. ([#5283](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5283))

#### Fixed

- **Live agents no longer read as "no agent":** a Herdr agent that finished its turn and waits at its prompt now counts as live on the Flywheel page and in `pan flywheel status`, and a handoff conversation or a queued quality gate counts as the issue's work in progress. ([#5283](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5283))
