#### Improved

- **CLIProxy upgraded to v8.0.71:** Overdeck now writes the CLIProxy config in the v8 layout, keeps a conversation on one account (session affinity), turns on the management API on localhost with a key in `~/.overdeck/cliproxy/management-key`, and keeps Codex-aliased model names off Claude accounts. Your `config.extra.yaml` keeps working: legacy keys are converted. Run `pan restart --cliproxy` once after updating to move a running proxy to v8.
