#### New

- **All projects deck:** pick **All projects** in the project switcher, or the new This project / All projects switch under Conversations, to list every project's conversations in one place, grouped by project. Ctrl+Click (Cmd+Click on macOS), the row's Beside button, or dragging a row onto a pane opens a conversation beside the ones on screen, up to a grid of four, each with its own composer; Layout presets and a "Make this the only pane" button rearrange them, and the deck and its layout survive a reload. ([#5343](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5343))
