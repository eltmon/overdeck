#### New

- **Strike PRs merge on their own:** an open strike PR with green checks now gets one automatic quick review per head. When the review approves it, the merge train schedules it ahead of the queue with the usual 5-minute cancel window, even while the train batches other work. A strike the reviewer flags stops, with the review on its PR. A strike in a project that holds for UAT still waits for your Merge click. ([#5276](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5276))
