#### New

- **Keyboard shortcuts sheet:** press `?` anywhere outside a text field to see every dashboard shortcut. Press `N` on the Command Deck to start a new conversation. ([#5287](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5287))

#### Improved

- **Tab shortcuts on Linux and Windows:** switch Stage tabs with `Ctrl+Alt+1` to `Ctrl+Alt+9` and close one with `Ctrl+Alt+W`, because the browser keeps `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+9` and `Ctrl+W`. macOS keeps `⌘1` to `⌘9` and `⌘W`. ([#5287](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/5287))
