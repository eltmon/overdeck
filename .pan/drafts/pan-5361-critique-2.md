## blocks-the-design: A single burst can flip the verdict

[PRD](/home/eltmon/Projects/overdeck/workspaces/feature-pan-5361/.pan/drafts/pan-5361.md), line 63, requires that one burst in any window cannot move the verdict across the budget; line 82 repeats that min-of-3 survives two bursts. The stated estimator does not guarantee this. With baseline `[6,8,10]`, idle `[36,37,38]`, and budget 30, the net is 30 (pass). Add one +65 burst to the first idle window and the idle counts become `[101,37,38]`; the net is 31 (fail). I reproduced those minima with Node. The named tests at lines 135-137 put bursts only in cases where the unchanged minimum happens to preserve the net. Smallest change: state the estimator's statistical benefit and residual near-budget risk in FR-1 and Decision 1, and test a burst on a unique minimum near the budget. If exact one-burst immunity is required, choose an estimator that satisfies it before implementation.

## blocks-the-design: Attribution double counts live children

[PRD](/home/eltmon/Projects/overdeck/workspaces/feature-pan-5361/.pan/drafts/pan-5361.md), line 68, adds traced child-process calls to growth in live tree tasks and labels the result `dashboard-tree` once it reaches half the excess. A child spawned during the window and still alive at the end contributes once to each term: the existing tracer logs its spawn ([script](/home/eltmon/Projects/overdeck/workspaces/feature-pan-5361/scripts/bare-host-smoke.sh), lines 510-517), while the proposed `/proc` snapshot includes it in task growth (PRD lines 157-179). For an excess of 40 PIDs consisting of 10 such children and 30 host PIDs, the formula reports `tracedPids=10`, `growth=10`, and `dashboard-tree`, although the dashboard accounts for only 10 of 40. This is a wrong diagnostic on a Linux failure. Smallest change: remove overlap before summing the two signals, or use a conservative bound such as `max(tracedPids, growth)` and describe the remaining uncertainty. Add a test with a traced child that remains live across the end snapshot.
