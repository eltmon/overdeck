## blocks-the-design: Cached target can falsely pass a merge

The PRD's FR-2 and D3a (lines 50 and 74) allow row 4 to pass from `origin/<target>` after `git fetch` fails, on the stated but unenforced assumption that the target is protected and never rewound. `resolveTargetComparison()` is used by `verifyConventionBranchMerged()` in `src/lib/lifecycle/verify-merged.ts`, lines 27–41 and 210–214; `getRepoTargetBranch()` accepts a configured `pr_target` or `default_branch` without a protection check in `src/lib/project-repos.ts`, lines 56–66. A cached `origin/main` at C2, a current target at C1 after a rewind or remote change, and a feature tip at C2 make the proposed ancestry check pass while the feature is absent from the current target. The existing local-main fallback would miss when local main is C1. This new pass can permit close-out teardown; a note in the successful result cannot prevent it. Make a failed-fetch fallback diagnostic-only unless an independent current-target witness confirms the target head, or enforce and test the protected, unchanged-remote precondition before accepting a pass.
