<Note>
Session Vault and device pairing are available today and free. The hosted services and
every price on this page are **planned**: nothing here can be bought yet. We publish the
prices now, with the reasoning, so you can see how we got there and tell us where we are wrong.
</Note>

Overdeck runs on your own machine. Your agents, workspaces and conversations live there.
**Overdeck Anywhere** is the set of features that let you and your work leave that machine.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Continue anywhere" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Pick up an agent conversation on a different machine, even when the first one is switched off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reach your Overdeck" icon="tower-broadcast">
    Get to your Overdeck from a laptop, phone or another service without opening ports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Know when you're needed" icon="bell">
    Get a notification when an agent needs you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share a live session" icon="users">

The plan lives in the Overdeck Anywhere epic,
[PAN-2350](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/2350). "Overdeck Anywhere" and
"Session Vault" are working names.

## What stays free and open source

<Check>
These are not trials and do not expire. Self-hosting has no feature gate, and we do not plan
to add one.

- **Overdeck itself**, including every feature that runs on your own machines.
- **All of Session Vault Phase A**: the `pan vault` commands, the git and directory
  backends, end-to-end encryption, full version history, and resume from any saved
  version. Your vault can be as large as your own backend allows.
- **Per-machine servers, federation and SSH hosts.** Connecting your own machines to each
  other needs no account and no subscription.
- **Leaving.** When the hosted vault launches, `pan vault migrate` (planned) will copy
  every record out of it to a backend you run, whether or not your subscription is active.
</Check>

## Roadmap

Most of Anywhere is open-source software you run yourself. The paid part comes last and
consists of hosted services.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Session Vault, Phase A">
    **Shipped (v0.64.0). Free, open source.**

    Saves agent conversations (Claude Code and Codex) as versioned, encrypted records on a
    backend you choose: a private git remote or a directory. Browse every saved conversation
    from any of your machines and continue one from any saved version.
    [PAN-2609](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/2609)
  </Step>
  <Step title="One Overdeck server per machine">
    **Partly shipped. Free, open source.**

    Shipped: machine identity, pairing and device sessions. Planned: a list of your machines
    with an environment switcher, advertised endpoints, then SSH hosts and remote servers.
    [PAN-3762](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/3762),
    [PAN-4402](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4402),
    [PAN-4403](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4403),
    [PAN-3863](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/3863),
    [PAN-4404](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4404)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notifications, remote access and a mobile web app">
    **Planned. Free, open source.**

    Needs-you notifications sent to a notification service you configure, remote dashboard
    access through a tunnel you control, and a mobile web app.
    [PAN-2354](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/2354),
    [PAN-2352](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/2352),
    [PAN-2355](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/2355)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remote agent workspaces on Fly.io">
    **Planned. Free, open source; you pay Fly directly.**

    Remote agent workspaces on Fly.io that you run yourself.
    [PAN-1775](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/1775),
    [PAN-1676](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/1676)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hosted services on overdeck.ai">
    **Account service built (invite-only); the rest planned. This is the paid plan above.**

    An account, the subscription, the hosted Session Vault, then the relay (reach your
    machines from anywhere, with hosted push notifications delivered through it), then shared
    sessions. The account service is built and invite-only: testers are added by the
    operator, and the hosted vault cap is 5 GB per tester.

    The server code for these services is published in
    [`services/`](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/tree/main/services) under the
    [Functional Source License](https://fsl.software) (FSL-1.1-MIT): you can read, run,
    modify and self-host it, but not offer it as a competing commercial service, and each
    release becomes MIT two years after it ships. Everything else in Overdeck stays MIT.
    [PAN-4293](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4293),
    [PAN-4294](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4294),
    [PAN-4297](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4297),
    [PAN-2356](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/2356),
    [PAN-658](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/658)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hosted agent capacity">
    **Planned. Billed separately by usage.**

    Your agents running on machines we manage.
    [PAN-4298](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4298)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How we arrived at these prices

The full reasoning, section by section. Open any one that interests you.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. What comparable products charge" icon="scale-balanced">
    We looked at two groups, with prices as published in September 2026.

    **Products that save or move AI coding sessions.** Most of them do it for free, or as
    part of a subscription you already buy for model access. Claude Code's Remote Control,
    included with Claude subscriptions, controls a session that is still running on its
    original machine. None of the products we found sell session storage by the gigabyte,
    and none encrypt agent transcripts with a key only the user holds.

    **Paid sync or hosted add-ons on top of a free or open-source core:**

    | Product | Price | Notes |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | [Obsidian Sync](https://obsidian.md/sync) | Standard &#36;4/mo annual or &#36;5 monthly (1 GB); Plus &#36;8/mo annual or &#36;10 monthly (10 GB) | Encrypted, versioned sync for a free app. The closest analogue. |
    | [Raycast Pro](https://www.raycast.com/pricing) | &#36;10/mo or &#36;96/yr | Includes cloud sync |
    | [Termius Pro](https://termius.com/pricing) | &#36;10/mo billed annually | Adds personal cloud vault sync |
    | [Zed Pro](https://zed.dev/pricing) | &#36;10/mo | |
    | [ngrok Hobbyist](https://ngrok.com/pricing) | &#36;10/mo, less billed annually | |
    | [Bitwarden Premium](https://bitwarden.com/pricing/) | &#36;19.80/yr | Server is open source and self-hostable |
    | [Atuin](https://atuin.sh/sync-shell-history/) | Hosted sync free | Server is open source; client-side encryption |
    | [iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108047) | 200 GB for &#36;2.99/mo | Consumer storage anchor |

    Two conclusions:

    - **&#36;8 a month billed annually, or &#36;10 monthly, is the settled price for an individual
      pro plan** on a developer tool. Obsidian Sync Plus sits exactly there with 10 GB. We
      offer 100 GB at the same price, because storing it costs us very little (next section).
    - **Nobody should pay per gigabyte for this.** Consumer cloud storage costs about &#36;1.50
      per 100 GB a month, so a per-gigabyte price above that would feel wrong. The price has
      to be for the workflow, not the bytes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2. What it costs us to store your vault" icon="database">
    **Volume.** A heavy user running many agents at once writes about 13 GB of raw
    transcripts a month. Transcripts compress well: zstd at level 3 shrank a sample of real
    transcripts by about 3.2x. That heavy user therefore adds about 4 GB of stored data a
    month and would take about two years to fill 100 GB. A typical single-agent user writes
    far less; we estimate 0.1 to 0.6 GB of stored data a month, but we have not measured it
    yet.

    **Storage.** The hosted vault is planned on Cloudflare R2 at &#36;0.015 per GB-month, with
    no egress fee, which matters because restoring a conversation on a new machine is a
    download. Durable Objects hold each vault's version pointers.

    **Requests.** A heavy user with ten agents saving every five minutes makes about 21,000
    writes and 50,000 reads a month. On R2 with Workers and Durable Objects that costs about
    &#36;0.16 a month.

    All-in cost per user per month:

    | Stored | Typical user | Heavy user |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | 1 GB | &#36;0.04 | &#36;0.18 |
    | 5 GB | &#36;0.10 | &#36;0.24 |
    | 20 GB | &#36;0.32 | &#36;0.46 |
    | 50 GB | &#36;0.77 | &#36;0.91 |
    | 100 GB | &#36;1.52 | &#36;1.66 |

    A free user costs us at most about 4 cents a month, so a free hosted tier is affordable.
    The fixed platform cost is small (Cloudflare Workers has a &#36;5 monthly minimum). The real
    cost of running this is support time: lost keys, billing questions and sync bugs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. Payment fees" icon="receipt">
    Creem charges 3.9% plus &#36;0.40 per successful charge, with no extra fee for international
    cards. Payouts to us cost the higher of &#36;7 or 1% of each payout. The fixed &#36;0.40 is
    what shapes the plan:

    | Charge | Fee | Share of the price |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | &#36;5 a month | &#36;0.60 | 11.9% |
    | &#36;10 a month | &#36;0.79 | 7.9% |
    | &#36;60 a year | &#36;2.74 | 4.6% |
    | &#36;96 a year | &#36;4.14 | 4.3% |

    In countries with VAT or GST the fee is charged on the tax-inclusive amount, so its share
    of our revenue there is somewhat higher.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. What the price pays for" icon="hammer">
    Storage and requests for a user at the full 100 GB cap cost us about &#36;1.70 a month, and
    a typical user costs well under that. Creem's fees take about &#36;4 of a &#36;96 annual plan.
    The rest of the price pays for building and maintaining Overdeck, supporting subscribers,
    and the parts of Anywhere that are not built yet.

    The relay and push notifications will add cost that we have not modeled yet. We will
    build that cost model before the relay leaves beta. Hosted agent compute is the one thing
    whose cost scales directly with use, which is why it is billed by usage instead of being
    included.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5. Why annual billing" icon="calendar">
    Because of the fixed &#36;0.40 per charge, a monthly plan loses about three times as much of
    each dollar to fees as an annual one. We still offer monthly at &#36;10 for people who want
    to try it without committing, but the annual price is 20% lower (&#36;96 against &#36;120), the
    same discount Raycast and Obsidian use. The founding price is annual only for the same
    reason.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="6. Why the vault is not sold on its own" icon="box">
    A standalone "encrypted backup of your transcripts" competes with free tools and with
    Obsidian Sync Standard at &#36;4 a month, and at &#36;3 to &#36;5 a month card fees would take 12%
    to 17% of it. Its honest price would be low, and most of it would go to fees.

    What the vault does that nothing else does is let your work follow you: continue on your
    laptop while your desktop is off, from any saved version, across harnesses, with a key
    only you hold. That is the same promise as the relay, push notifications and, later,
    shared sessions. So the vault anchors one Anywhere subscription instead of being a
    separate product.

    At launch only the vault exists, so we would be charging a bundle price for one feature.
    The founding price addresses that: &#36;60 a year, locked for as long as you stay
    subscribed, until the relay ships or 500 people have joined.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="7. Why self-hosting stays ungated" icon="lock-open">
    - It is the promise the project was built on. Weakening the free git backend would break
      that promise, and the promise is worth more than the subscriptions it might add.
    - It removes lock-in. You can start self-hosted, move to the hosted vault, and move back
      with `pan vault migrate` once it ships (planned with the hosted vault,
      [PAN-4297](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/4297)).
    - It does not take much away from the paid plan. Encrypted records cannot be delta
      compressed, so a heavy user outgrows a free git host's recommended repository size in a
      few months. The people who value the vault most are the ones most likely to want
      someone else to run it. Bitwarden and Atuin both publish their servers and still run
      popular hosted services.
    - The hosted services themselves are source-available under FSL-1.1-MIT rather than MIT.
      That stops someone reselling them as a competing service; it does not limit running
      them yourself, and each release turns MIT after two years.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="8. Why a free hosted tier and not a trial" icon="gift">
    Every direct alternative stores sessions for free, so a time-limited trial would lose the
    "try it" comparison. The 1 GB free tier costs us about 4 cents per user a month.
    Transcripts only grow, so the free tier fills up on its own for anyone who uses it
    heavily, and no feature needs to be held back to encourage upgrades. Version history is
    never gated, because resuming from any saved version is the point of the vault.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="9. Why there is no team plan yet" icon="user-group">
    Every record is encrypted with a single key that one person holds. Sharing a vault with a
    team needs a key-sharing design, and it depends on shared sessions
    ([PAN-658](https://github.com/eltmon/overdeck/issues/658)). We will not publish a team
    price until that design exists.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What we still need to learn

<Info>
- How much a typical user actually stores. The vault collects no telemetry, so we plan a
  local `pan vault stats` command and will ask early users to share its output.
- What the relay costs to run per user.
- Whether people will pay &#36;96 a year, and &#36;60 at the founding price. We plan a waitlist
  before launch.
